# GarudaOne: Market Positioning & Pricing

## The Indian Drone Market Reality (2026)
- **DJI import ban:** Active since Feb 2022. No authorized retail in India. Grey market prices: ₹90K–₹1.2L+ with zero warranty.
- **Local competitors (IZI etc.):** ₹32K–₹70K range. Often "fake 4K" with poor sensor quality.
- **Sub-250g advantage:** No DGCA license, no UIN, no insurance required for recreational use.

## GarudaOne Positioning
**"DJI-level 4K cinematic quality, driven by a Voice-Controlled AI Brain, Made in India."**

### Target Price: ₹49,999–₹54,999

### Why This Price Point
| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **vs Grey-Market DJI** | Nearly half the price. Full warranty. Legal. |
| **vs Local competitors** | Premium positioning with true Sony IMX678 4K. Side-by-side footage comparison wins. |
| **Unit economics** | ~₹10K BOM at scale → ~80% gross margin |
| **Creator psychology** | Under ₹50K = tax-deductible business expense for professional creators |

### Target Customer
- Indian YouTube creators (100K+ subscribers)
- Instagram Reels / content creators
- Wedding/event videographers
- Real estate / architecture showcase
- Travel vloggers

### Competitive Moats
1. **AI Moat:** No drone in India (or globally) has an on-device Liquid Foundation Model with voice control.
2. **Legal Moat:** Made in India, DGCA compliant, 1-year domestic warranty.
3. **Quality Moat:** True 4K with Sony IMX678 sensor crushes "upscaled 4K" competitors.
4. **Price Moat:** Half the cost of grey-market DJI with better AI autonomy.
