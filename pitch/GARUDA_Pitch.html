The Problem

Drones don't understand their own physics

A drone in turbulent wind burns more power. A hot battery loses capacity. A worn motor creates asymmetric thrust. Today's drones don't track any of this — they find out when they crash.

No energy awareness

Battery drains unpredictably under wind load. The drone doesn't know it can't make it home until it's already too late.

Blind to atmosphere

Air density drops at altitude, turbulence varies with terrain, temperature affects everything. None of this is modeled.

No component health

Motor bearings wear, batteries heat up, propellers degrade. There's no way to detect partial failure before it becomes total.

We need a system that continuously estimates the drone's physical state, predicts failures, quantifies how certain it is, and makes safety decisions it can justify.

Our Approach

Five physics modules running in a loop at 10 Hz

Each module feeds into the next. The decision engine sits on top and evaluates the complete picture every 100ms.

Physics Models

Environment: atmosphere + wind

ICAO Standard Atmosphere

Altitude-dependent temperature, pressure, and air density:

T(h) = 288.15 − 0.0065 · h
ρ(h) = 1.225 · (T / 288.15)4.256

Air density drops with altitude — this directly affects how much power the motors need to produce the same thrust. We recalculate this every tick.

Dryden Wind Turbulence

Stochastic gusts modeled as an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process:

x(t+dt) = x(t)·e−dt/τ + σ√(1−e−2dt/τ)·N(0,1)

Wind is random but correlated in time (τ = 4s). This is the standard model used in aerospace (MIL-F-8785C). The vertical axis has 60% lower turbulence than horizontal — matches real atmospheric behavior.

Disturbance scenarios we can inject live

Crosswind gust: 12 m/s sudden lateral wind

Temperature spike: +20°C (flying over a heat island)

Downdraft: 5 m/s vertical shear (microburst)

Wind shear layer: Altitude-dependent velocity gradient

These aren't hypothetical — each one is a single function call that changes the simulation state, and the decision engine reacts within the same tick.

Physics Models

Vehicle health + energy forecasting

Battery: thermal ODE + Coulomb counting

dT/dt = (I²R − h·A·ΔT) / (m·Cₚ)

The battery heats from internal resistance (I²R) and cools from airflow (h scales with airspeed). We track the thermal state every tick because LiPo capacity drops with temperature — a hot battery gives you less flight time than the voltage suggests.

V = 3S · (3.3 + 0.9·SoC0.8) − I·Rint

Real LiPo discharge curve. Parameters match our actual 3S/450mAh pack (0.08Ω internal resistance).

Motor degradation

Each of the 4 motors is tracked independently. Bearing wear degrades efficiency at 15%/1000h. The delta between best and worst motor (imbalance) is a key safety signal — asymmetric thrust is dangerous.

Power: Actuator Disk Momentum Theory

Phover = √(T³ / 2ρA) · κ / η
Pfwd = Phover·f(v) + ½ρCdAv³

Hover power depends on thrust, air density, disk area (4× 2-inch props), and rotor efficiency. Forward flight adds parasitic drag. All parameters come from our real hardware config.

Safe operating envelope

Computed dynamically every tick based on current health + environment:

  • Max altitude (density-limited)
  • Max speed (rotor-efficiency-limited)
  • Max range at cruise speed
  • Can we reach the target, come home, and still have 30s reserve?

Uncertainty Quantification

Kalman filter: physics meets sensors

The physics models predict what should happen. The sensors measure what actually happened. The Kalman filter figures out the best estimate and tells you how confident it is.

How it works, every tick:

Predict (from physics model): x̂ = model_prediction(alt, speed, wind) P += Q · dt ← uncertainty grows Update (from noisy sensor): K = P / (P + R) ← Kalman gain x̂ += K · (z − x̂) ← fuse measurement P = (1 − K) · P ← uncertainty shrinks Output: estimate ± 2σ ← 95% confidence band

Autonomous Decisions

6-tier hierarchical safety engine

Evaluates from most severe to least. Stops at the first tier that triggers. Every decision carries an evidence trail.

ABORT
Emergency landing — immediate Battery ≥ 60°C · rotor efficiency < 30% · structural stress exceeded · endurance < 45s
RTB
Return to base Not enough energy for RTH · SoC ≤ 18% · battery ≥ 50°C · motor degradation · wind ≥ 8.5 m/s
ALT
Change altitude Air density too thin at current height · vertical downdraft ≥ 3 m/s
SLOW
Reduce speed Turbulence intensity ≥ 0.50 · crosswind ≥ 5.5 m/s · rotor imbalance ≥ 0.15
ROUTE
Modify trajectory Headwind ≥ 60% of airspeed — reroute to save energy
GO
Continue mission All parameters nominal. Confidence: 96%.

Every decision record includes: trigger parameter + value + threshold, confidence score, physics snapshot, and alternatives that were considered but rejected.

Innovation Beyond the Brief

"Hey Garuda" — voice-controlled digital twin

We built a fully offline voice pipeline. No cloud, no internet — everything runs on-device. Think Siri, but for a drone, and it never phones home.

3

Intent routing — 3 tiers: keyword match (<5 ms), Needle 26M (<20 ms), LFM-2.5 (200 ms)

Voice feedback — Piper TTS responds: "Starting orbit mode"

Most commands resolve in under 20 ms. Complex natural language goes to the LFM for full understanding.

Only the owner + 2 registered voices can command the drone. Speaker verification rejects unauthorized commands — a real security layer for deployment.
